Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?+
No! You can start your 7-day free trial with just an email address or Google account. No payment info required until you decide to continue.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes, absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. There are no cancellation fees or locked-in contracts.
What happens to my data if I cancel?+
Your trade data stays safe and accessible for 30 days after cancellation, giving you time to export a CSV backup. After 30 days, data is permanently deleted per our privacy policy.
Which prop firm CSV formats do you support?+
We support imports from TopstepX, Apex Trader Funding, FTMO, MyFundedFutures, Earn2Trade, BluSky, Tradeify, Topstep, and most other platforms that export standard trade history CSVs.
Is my trading data private and secure?+
100%. Your data is stored encrypted in Google Firebase and is never sold, shared, or used to train AI models. You own your data completely.
Can I switch between monthly and annual?+
Yes. You can upgrade to annual at any time and we'll prorate the remaining time from your current monthly plan.
