Questions & answers
Is there a free trial? +
Yes — Starter is free forever with no time limit. Use TradeDesk indefinitely at no cost. Pro unlocks the advanced features when you're ready to go deeper.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period — no questions, no friction.
Is my trade data private? +
Your data is stored in your own Firebase account and is never shared or sold. TradeDesk does not run ads or monetize your trading data in any way.
Does it work on mobile? +
Yes. TradeDesk is fully responsive with a dedicated bottom navigation bar for mobile. Works on any device and any browser — no app install required.
Which broker CSV formats are supported? +
Tradovate, NinjaTrader, IBKR, Webull, TD Ameritrade, Schwab, E*TRADE, Tastytrade, TradeStation, Apex, Rithmic, and generic CSV. More formats added regularly.
What happens to my data if I downgrade? +
Your data is never deleted. If you downgrade from Pro to Free, your history is preserved — you just lose access to the Pro views until you upgrade again.
